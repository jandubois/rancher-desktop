## Assessment

Failed jobs fell to 276 from 399, and the whole drop is the quarantined BATS workflow, which went from 76 failed of 96 jobs on 10-28 to 9 of 130 on 10-29 and 10 of 130 on 10-30 after #11162 repaired it. BATS still fails every run, so it stays quarantined, but those last two runs failed 17 jobs on macOS, 2 on Windows and none on Linux, few enough to read one by one. Outside the quarantine, 29 of 2,106 jobs failed (1.4%, from 1.1%), and 9 of the 29 came from electron-updater 6.8.10 breaking `yarn build` on #11224 until the PR was fixed and merged on 10-26. No fixed flake came back, and nothing that merged broke `main`, because its 14 failures were 5 `got` patch Dependabot scans (waiting on yarnpkg/berry#7282), 2 Dependabot backend failures and 7 flakes, 6 of which passed on a re-run. The two flakes to watch are lost e2e runners (6 of 250 jobs, 2.4%) and `win-signing-artifact-timeout`, which hit 5 of the last 82 signing jobs after 0 of 98 in the three weeks before; its monthly count has moved between 1 and 7 since April, so I read that as a burst and not a trend. The fix to make this week is adding `setup-go` to `codeql.yaml`, and it doesn't show up as a failure, because `Analyze (go)` passed 114 of 115 runs while the runner's Go 1.24.13 rejected `go.work` (which asks for 1.27.3) and CodeQL extracted only `bats/go.mod`.

<details>
<summary>How this report is built</summary>

`ci-sweep weekly --week 2026-W44` over `sweep.db`, one row per **failed job** — a run can fail on one matrix leg out of many, so the job is the unit.

- **Known flake** — a job whose log matched a `matchers.yaml` pattern. Pattern matching only, no LLM.
- **One flake per job** — when several matchers fire, the job counts under the one whose surface appears **earliest** in the log, because the first failure is the root and what follows is fallout: a suite killed at its wall-clock cap logs a failed wait on the way out. The others are recorded but not counted, and only a true tie is left uncounted.
- **Proven flake** — the identical job passed on a re-run of the same commit, so the code cannot be at fault.
- **Unrecognized** — no matcher fired. On `main` it earns analysis; on a PR branch it earns only a count, because PR failures are dev breakage, not CI health.
- **No log** — the log expired or was never uploaded, so the sweep could not classify the failure. A data gap, not a clean bill.
- **What failed** — for a known flake, the matcher's summary; otherwise the failing test and command, or the job error for a failure that never reached its tests. `--fetch` names the rest, at one download each.
- Counts cover only jobs the sweep recorded. A period never swept reads as zero failures.

</details>

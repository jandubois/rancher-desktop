## Assessment

The failure rate rose to 2.9% from 1.4% (46 of 1,559 jobs outside the quarantine), and 19 of the 46 come from one real break; without it the rate is 1.8%, close to the 4-week average of 1.7%. Since Electron 45 merged (#11239, 11-03 17:05Z), `Test Signing (Windows)` has failed on every push to `main`, 19 of 19 through Friday, because Electron ships a WER DLL that `build/signing-config-win.yaml` doesn't list. The first 14 logs name `electron_wer.dll`, and once electron-builder 26.18.1 (#11303, 11-05 23:50Z) renamed it, the last 5 name `Rancher Desktop_wer.dll`, but the yaml lists only RD2's `Rancher Desktop 2_wer.dll`. All 19 regression candidates are this break, and draft PR #11312 adds the missing line. `win-signing-artifact-timeout`, last week's flake to watch, is silent, but that tells us nothing, because since 11-03 every signing job on `main` has died in `findFilesToSign`, before the WiX step where most of its timeouts happened. The rest of `main` is the `got` patch Dependabot scan (5, still waiting on yarnpkg/berry#7282), one `check-update-versions` dispatch that failed all three attempts at its vault login during the 11-02 outage, and 3 Windows e2e jobs that lost their runner; across all branches that happened to 7 of 192 Windows e2e jobs (3.6%), against 6 of 256 in W44.

<details>
<summary>How this report is built</summary>

`ci-sweep weekly --week 2026-W45` over `sweep.db`, one row per **failed job** — a run can fail on one matrix leg out of many, so the job is the unit.

- **Known flake** — a job whose log matched a `matchers.yaml` pattern. Pattern matching only, no LLM.
- **One flake per job** — when several matchers fire, the job counts under the one whose surface appears **earliest** in the log, because the first failure is the root and what follows is fallout: a suite killed at its wall-clock cap logs a failed wait on the way out. The others are recorded but not counted, and only a true tie is left uncounted.
- **Proven flake** — the identical job passed on a re-run of the same commit, so the code cannot be at fault.
- **Unrecognized** — no matcher fired. On `main` it earns analysis; on a PR branch it earns only a count, because PR failures are dev breakage, not CI health.
- **No log** — the log expired or was never uploaded, so the sweep could not classify the failure. A data gap, not a clean bill.
- **What failed** — for a known flake, the matcher's summary; otherwise the failing test and command, or the job error for a failure that never reached its tests. `--fetch` names the rest, at one download each.
- Counts cover only jobs the sweep recorded. A period never swept reads as zero failures.

</details>
